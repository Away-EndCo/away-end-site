The Story

Why Away End Exists

Every away day starts the same way. You open five different tabs — the club website, Trainline, Google Maps, a Reddit thread from 2019, and a Facebook group that hasn't been updated in three years. You piece together a rough plan and hope for the best.

Away End was built to fix that. One place. Everything you need. Written by someone who's been there.

The founder is a lifelong Manchester City fan who has followed the club to grounds across the UK and Europe. Based in Switzerland, there's a particular understanding of what it takes to plan a trip from further afield — the extra research, the unfamiliar train systems, the question of where on earth you're supposed to drink before a game in a city you don't know.

That experience is what drives every guide we publish. Not SEO checklists. Not AI-generated filler. Real, verified, fan-first information that you can actually rely on matchday.

Accuracy is the brand. We'd rather publish fewer guides that are right than more guides that aren't. Every pub recommendation is verified. Every avoid warning is genuine. If we're not confident, we say so.

How It Works

How We Build Our Guides

01
Verified Sources Only
Every guide starts with club official away fan pages, footballgroundguide.com, and recent Google Maps reviews from real visitors. We cross-reference everything and never rely on a single source.
02
Fan Community Input
We monitor fan forums and club subreddits for real-world experience. If fans are reporting a pub has gone home-only, we update the guide. Community knowledge is our most valuable asset.
03
Honest Over Helpful
We'd rather tell you a ground has a poor away end than pretend it's fine. We'd rather say there are no good pubs near the ground than recommend somewhere that gets you in trouble. Honesty is the brand.
